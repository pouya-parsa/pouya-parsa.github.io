About me

I am an M.S. student at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities. My work focuses on large language models, LLM memory, and systems that can preserve, retrieve, and reason over useful context across long interactions.

I am especially interested in memory-augmented LLMs: how models can maintain persistent knowledge, decide what to remember, recover relevant experiences, and use memory to support more reliable reasoning.

Publications

Visual Distribution Anchoring for Efficient Prompt Tuning

Pouya Parsa, Raoof Zare Moayedi, and Seongjin Choi. arXiv:2607.28967. Preprint, July 2026.

VDA turns a disjoint unlabeled target pool into class-specific visual prototypes and fuses them with frozen semantic classifiers. The primary matched result improves mean accuracy from 65.82% to 69.21% while requiring no target-side optimization or test-query access.

Explore the project page · Read the paper

Can the Cloud Drive? Infrastructure Feasibility of Offloading Autonomous Driving Across 5G and 6G

Pouya Parsa, Kawon Han, and Seongjin Choi. arXiv:2607.09045, 2026.

The study shows why near-term cloud VLA inference is often limited by GPU memory bandwidth—even after 5G/6G can carry the workload—and when shared cloud GPUs can reduce cost.

Explore the interactive article · Read the paper
